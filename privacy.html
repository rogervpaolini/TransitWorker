A direct explanation of what information this static website may receive when you choose to contact the desk.

PRIVACY / 06

Simple by design. Respectful by default.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, and the details of your enquiry. We only use that information to understand and respond to your message.

How information is used

Information is not sold or used for unrelated advertising. We may retain correspondence long enough to respond, keep a record of the conversation, or meet basic administrative needs.

External links

This website may link to third-party services. Those sites have their own privacy practices and terms, which should be reviewed when you follow a link.

Questions

For a privacy question or a request about information you have shared, use the Contact page and include enough context for us to locate the conversation.
